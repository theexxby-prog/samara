About Me

Hospitality is a craft,
not just a job.

I'm Samara Mehta, a hospitality professional and kinesiology student at the University of Massachusetts Amherst. My journey in food service began behind an espresso machine and has grown into leading teams, managing operations, and creating the kind of guest experiences that keep people coming back.

What started as a love for coffee quickly became a passion for the entire hospitality ecosystem — the rhythm of a busy café, the satisfaction of a perfectly crafted order, and the challenge of keeping a team motivated through the rush.

My background in kinesiology sharpens my understanding of how people move, work, and perform — making me a more effective team leader and a more thoughtful operator.
