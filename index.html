About Me

Where design meets code

I'm Samara — a creative designer and front-end developer who believes that the best digital products live at the intersection of aesthetics and engineering. Every pixel matters, and so does every line of code.

My background spans both visual design (Figma, Adobe Creative Suite) and modern web development (React, Tailwind, JavaScript). Whether I'm crafting a brand identity, wireframing a user flow, or building a responsive web app, I bring the same meticulous attention to detail to everything I do.

Beyond screens, I pull real-world empathy from my time as a barista — learning that great experiences are about reading people, solving problems quickly, and never skimping on quality.
